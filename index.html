// Malaysian tech community

A community for Malaysia's engineers.

Engineers.MY supports the Malaysian tech scene. We help people and user groups find each other, share what they know and work on things together.

Join us on Discord What we do

What we do

01

Connecting people

Find local tech user groups and get them talking to each other.

02

Sharing knowledge

Learn, mentor and share advice, so beginners can get help from people with more experience.

03

Collaborating

Find people to work with on FOSS projects, community events or a business.

A safe, inclusive community

We want Engineers.MY to be safe and welcoming for everyone. Please read the Code of Conduct and follow it on Discord and at events.
